About

Hi! I'm a software engineer and game developer based in Boston, MA. I attended the University of Toronto for computer science with a focus on graphics and game development.


I have a passion for interaction design, storytelling, and accessibility in software. My journey into programming started with a love of art and animation, and I've since been driven to foster human connection and creativity with technology.


I also like music, swords, and bread.

Through my work I have significant experience with JavaScript, particularly with React, Ember, and Next.js. C++. I've worked in Unreal Engine, Unity, and Godot, along with proprietary C++ engines.


Accessibility one of my core design values. Technology should be approachable for everyone, especially given its growing necessity in everyday life, and I believe that prioritizing intuitive and joyful user experiences is critical for a more equitable future.

As a game developer, I aim to explore the impact of interactivity on storytelling and narrative design. My favorite games include Pathologic and Dishonored, and their focus on environment and decisionmaking.

In my spare time I play the viola in a handful of community orchestras and chamber ensembles. I also study the blade at a historical fencing club with a personal focus on rapier and dagger.


Otherwise I enjoy baking, watching movies, going to goth clubs, and studying Latin.
